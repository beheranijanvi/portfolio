About me

Hi there, I am an interaction designer born and raised in Jalgaon, Maharashtra currently studying at Anant National University.

A designer with way too much curiosity for one field currently diving deep between Speculative designing, Multi sensory design, service design and much more — basically where curiosity is meeting chaos. With a minor in Startup & Entrepreneurship, I'm wired to prototype wild ideas that might just work. Over the years, I understood that every little thing crossovers interaction and every interaction tells a story.

When I'm not ironing out the details of my assignments, I'm probably out there collecting stories, attending events, and finding inspiration in unexpected places — because sometimes, the best user research happens when you just explore. Being an early bird, i work my best at 6:30 a.m ;).
